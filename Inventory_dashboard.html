Three actions to take from this review

01 / ACQUISITIONS

Review lower-priced stock

Below AED 30,000: 30 Kavak cars versus 65 Cars24 tagged cars. Attrage accounts for 23 of the 35-car gap (5 versus 28).

Check Attrage buying costs and enquiries from interested buyers before adding stock. Buy a small test batch only if expected margin and buyer interest support it. Track enquiries, the share that become sales and time to sell.

02 / PRICING

Check the three Attrage examples

Two 2023 Attrage examples are AED 2,000 and AED 3,000 below similar-mileage peers. A 2025 example is AED 3,000 above its peer.

Verify full trim, GCC spec, condition, inclusions and gross margin. Test changes only where those checks support them; track margin and the share of enquiries that become sales.

03 / MERCHANDISING

Highlight makes with more choice

Kavak has 68 Volkswagen, Jeep and Peugeot cars available, versus 37 Cars24 tagged cars. Its median mileage is also lower: 53,285 versus 59,000 km.

Give these cars more visibility. Check enquiries and sales by model before buying more. Cars24 has a newer median model year overall: 2024 versus 2023.
